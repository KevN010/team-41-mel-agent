1  Problem statement

FarmerChat gives advice. It does not learn what happened next.

Digital Green's FarmerChat gives advice to farmers in Bihar. After the advice, FarmerChat gets no measured outcome.

Limit There is no real delivery. All data is synthetic.
  1. Decide which conversations get a follow-up survey.
  2. Write 1 to 3 tap questions in the farmer's language.
  3. Schedule the survey in a send window.
  4. Record the answers.
  5. Route bad outcomes back for help.
300farmers
6,999insights
150golden cases
183outcome records

Participant pack scale. Source: pack/README.txt

Three constraints

  • CostFew model tokens for each survey.
  • SecurityFarmer identity from the JWT only.
  • SimplicityFew parts to build and deploy.
